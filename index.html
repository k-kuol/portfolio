How I Work

Web Development

I design the database first, then build outward: plain HTML, CSS, and JS when that's enough, React and Django when it's not. I'd rather ship something that works than wait on the perfect stack.

Graphic Design

I start from what the material actually needs to do in the room: get funded, get remembered, get used, and design backward from there. A deck that never leaves the inbox doesn't count.

Social Media Management

I plan and post like there's an actual person behind the account, not a brand: captions that sound human, a consistent voice across every platform, and enough restraint to post only what's worth someone's time.

About Me

I build things because building is how I make sense of chaos. Somewhere between writing code, sketching a brand, and putting down lines that started as a diary entry, I stopped treating developer, designer, and writer as separate people, they're just different tools for the same instinct: turn a mess into something that works. I was nine when I left home during the war in South Sudan, and I've been figuring things out without a manual ever since. These days that's software, mentoring, and stories, often the same week. I'm not the fastest at any one, just the one who won't stop until it's done well.

Hello, I'm Kuol

Building software, designing visuals, and writing the words that resonate.

I'm Kuol Akech Riak, a Web Developer and Digital Communications Specialist based in Kigali, Rwanda. I design and build full-stack web applications, thinking through how they're structured before I ever touch the interface, then shipping products people actually enjoy using, and I pair that work with the visuals and content that make it land: sharp graphic design, brand systems, and writing that sounds like a person, not a template. Working across South Sudan, Rwanda, and beyond, I help individuals, nonprofits, and growing organizations turn scattered ideas into tools their audiences can actually use, and turn quiet work into stories people remember. I'm currently studying Software Engineering at the African Leadership University (ALU), building the same range of skills into everything I take on next.

Why I build

I build things because building is how I make sense of chaos. Somewhere between writing code, sketching a brand, and putting down lines that started as a diary entry, I stopped treating developer, designer, and writer as separate people, they're just different tools for the same instinct: turn a mess into something that works. I was nine when I left home during the war in South Sudan, and I've been figuring things out without a manual ever since. These days that's software, mentoring, and stories, often the same week. I'm not the fastest at any one, just the one who won't stop until it's done well.
